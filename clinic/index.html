    Which care do I need?

    Three questions, thirty seconds.

    A quick way to choose the right kind of appointment. It does not diagnose anything, and if it feels urgent, always call.

    Good to know

    Questions we hear every day.

    Do I need a referral to see a specialist?

    No. Book any specialist directly. If your insurer asks for a referral, our family doctors can write one the same day.

    Which insurance do you accept?

    Most major health insurers and employer schemes. Bring your card and we handle the claim; you only pay any excess.

    Can I bring my child to my own appointment?

    Of course. There's a supervised play corner by reception, and our nurses are very good at distracting toddlers.

    How do I get my results?

    In the app, usually within 24 hours. If anything needs attention, your doctor calls you rather than leaving it to a notification.

    Is there parking?

    Free parking for 40 cars behind the building, with two spaces reserved for patients with reduced mobility at the entrance.
